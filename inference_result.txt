Once upon a time in there was a big boat. Inside the island, it sailed on the sea. The captain loved to sail fast and go on adventures. He would swim very much that he could play with his friends. One day while they were playing, they decided to have an adventure together. They had many fun ideas for each other like pirates. But then, one pirate said "it's too dangerous!" Everyone agreed but when they got out of their boats, something strange happened! A mean fish swam over and tried to take them away from him. Sadly, everyone couldn't get back home without any more treasure. From this experience: always be careful around strangers because you may find your way."
